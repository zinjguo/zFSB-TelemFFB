#####################################
This is an alpha release of zTelem - 
A stripped down fork of VPForce's TelemFFB to work with zFSB force sensing joystick.
#####################################


The goal is to make the FFB spring dyanmics customizable for many supported games.

Due to lack of a gimbal in the force sensing base, the haptics a are provided by
a small rumble motor(ERM). 

All the periodic effects are emulated, and are there not to provide immersion, 
but haptic hints to what the plane is doing.
